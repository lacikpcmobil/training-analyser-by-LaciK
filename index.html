How to read this

CTL (fitness) builds over ~42 days. ATL (fatigue) reflects the last ~7 days. TSB = CTL − ATL. Positive TSB means you're fresh. Target TSB between −10 and +20 for race day.

The 80/20 rule

Elite endurance athletes do ~80% of training in Z1–Z2 and only ~20% hard. If your easy zone share is below 60% you may be overreaching.
