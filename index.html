How to read this

CTL builds fitness over ~42 days. ATL tracks fatigue over ~7 days. TSB = CTL − ATL. Positive TSB = fresh & ready. Target −10 to +20 on race day.

The 80/20 rule

Elite athletes do ~80% in Z1–Z2 (easy) and ~20% in Z3–Z5 (hard). If your easy share is below 60% you may be overreaching.
